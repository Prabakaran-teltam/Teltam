Our Story

About Teltam AI

Bridging language barriers with advanced neural network technologies that preserve structural context, tone, and cultural nuances.

Redefining Translation for a Connected World

Teltam was founded by a team of linguistic experts and computer scientists who noticed that traditional, phrase-based translation systems failed to capture context. Idioms were mistranslated, spelling systems were ignored, and technical definitions were lost.

By fine-tuning custom Large Language Models, Teltam recognizes sentences not just as arrays of words, but as carriers of culture, emotion, and technical instruction.

Our Vision

To build a world where anyone can read, speak, and write any language naturally, without friction.

Our Mission

To deliver highly precise, real-time context-aware translation tools accessible to individuals and enterprises alike.

System Architecture

How Teltam's Neural Pipelines Work

  • Tokenizer Optimization

    Sentence structures are split into specific semantic tokens rather than character boundaries, speeding up multi-byte translation (like Tamil, Chinese).

  • Context Window Attention

    Our models scan paragraphs concurrently, ensuring pronoun agreements and local expressions translate naturally across large files.

  • Phonetic Synthesizer

    Converts localized phonemes into IPA guidelines, then compiles audio outputs through advanced speech synthesis algorithms.
